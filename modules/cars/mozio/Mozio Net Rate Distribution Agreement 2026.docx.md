**0\.  Definitions**

As used herein, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

“API” means Mozio’s JSON or XML webservice that enables Company to integrate both search and booking of Provider inventory directly into Company’s website.

“Customer” means a consumer who uses Company’s websites or contact centers to shop for and/or purchase travel-related products and services.

“Profit” is defined as the sum of the commission negotiated from the provider, and any extra fees Mozio has decided to charge on top. Mozio’s commissions vary from 5-30%, and Mozio will constantly be experimenting with pricing, extra fees, discounts and more. The Profit will not be a constant number, as Mozio’s algorithms will be testing out price vs. conversion rate and many other factors, with the intent of increasing overall profitability of the partnership.

“Provider” means any of Mozio’s third party providers of shuttles, air porters, limos, taxis, helicopters, seaplanes and transit cards and ancillaries such as Parking, eSIMs or Lounges.

“Qualifying service” means a booking made with a Provider via the Service by a Customer either on using the White Label, or through the Mozio API with the Company’s API key.

“Deep Link” means a link to Mozio’s homepage or search page from one of Company’s websites. Company refers all traffic to Mozio with a reference URL, and Mozio is the merchant of record.

“White Label” means a branded company portal, hosted by Mozio, but branded with Company’s logo. Company refers all traffic to Mozio with a reference URL, and Mozio is the merchant of record.

 “Service” means Mozio’s solution that gives Company access to Mozio’s inventory of Providers, via the White Label, Deep Link and/or the API.

“3rd\-party Partner” is defined as any 3rd\-party company to whom Company re-sells the ability to provide Mozio’s ground transportation services.

“Net Rates” means the price returned by Mozio’s API that includes the amount payable by partner to Mozio, and does not include any commission or revenue share payable to Partner.

**1.2 Commissions Earned from Qualifying Service.** Company will earn a commission from any Qualifying Service when the Customer books a reservation on the Mozio white-label site, Deep Link, or through the Mozio API with the Company’s API key that is not subsequently cancelled.

**2\. Payment**

**2.1 Net Rates.** The company agrees to pay Mozio the price returned by Mozio’s API which does not include any commission or revenue share payable to Partner earned off of each Qualifying Service according to the terms set forth in the attached Exhibit A.

**2.2 Merchant of Record**  
**2.2.1 Mozio is Merchant of Record** Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Mozio will take payment and serve as Merchant of record for any Qualifying Service booked by Company using any Mozio solution (including API, Deep Linking and White Label). Mozio will provide Customer Service, including initiating refunds to the Customer in the event a refund is required (e.g. in the event of a cancellation of a Qualifying Service). In cases where Company and Mozio agree that Company will be Merchant of Record, company will provide Mozio with a mechanism (e.g. API callback) to initiate refunds to   
customers, in the event one is required.

**2.3 Settlement**   
**2.3.1 Settlement When Company is Merchant of Record** Whenever Company serves as Merchant of Record, 

1) Mozio will send an invoice monthly (by the 1st day of each month) for any Qualifying Services booked in the previous billing period. Additionally, Mozio will send a detailed report outlining the following for each Qualifying Service: (a) the price provided to Company via API.   
2) Company agrees to send payment via bank transfer for the sum of all Qualifying Services, less Company’s commission, 15 days after receipt of invoice.    
3) Any cancellations will be subtracted from invoices in the month that the Qualifying Service is cancelled.

**2.3.2 Settlement When Mozio is Merchant of Record**  
Whenever Mozio serves as Merchant of Record, Mozio and Company will settle payments as follows: 

1) Within 1 week of the end of each calendar month, Mozio will provide Company with a Report setting out (a) the price, (b) total Profit, and (c) Company’s share of the Profit for all Qualifying Services where travel occurred that were not cancelled prior to travel occurring.   
2) Company will send Mozio an invoice for Company’s share of Profits (as outlined in Exhibit A).  
3) Mozio will make payment via bank transfer within 15 days of receipt of invoice.   
4) Minimum Payment Threshold. Notwithstanding the foregoing, where the total amount payable by Mozio to Company for any settlement period is less than five hundred United States Dollars (USD $500), such amount shall not be paid for that period and shall instead be carried forward and accrued in the account until the total outstanding balance equals or exceeds USD $500, at which point payment shall be made in accordance with the terms above. Reporting shall continue to be provided on a monthly basis regardless of whether the payment threshold is met.

Each Party will provide correct and relevant banking information to the other Party to be able to facilitate the settlement procedures described above.

**2.4 Taxes** Net proceeds paid to Mozio for all Qualifying Services shall include all applicable sales, use and excise taxes, and any other similar taxes, duties and charges of any kind imposed by any national, state or local governmental entity (“Taxes”). Mozio shall be responsible for accurately notifying Company of, collecting and remitting to Providers for payment to the appropriate taxing authorities, any and all such Taxes. 

However, in no event shall Mozio pay or be responsible for the payment or reimbursement to Company of any taxes (including, but not limited to, income, franchise or capital taxes or similar fees or taxes) measured by or against Company’s income or property.

**2.5 Tracking of Sales.** Mozio will track all sales made to Customers who purchase Qualifying Services using special links from Company site to the Mozio site. Mozio will send Company reports summarizing this aggregate and anonymous sales activity on a monthly basis. To permit accurate tracking, reporting, and commission accrual, Company must ensure that the special links between Company site and Mozio’s site are properly formatted and functioning.
